What to check before ordering

  1. Check the seller listing

    Open the product page and locate the original seller link if it is available. Paste that seller link into your agent’s order form and confirm the selected color, size, price and stock.

  2. Compare measurements in centimeters

    For clothes, measure a similar garment laid flat and compare chest width, sleeve length and body length. For shoes, compare foot length with the seller’s chart and check whether the chart lists foot or insole length.

  3. Review warehouse QC photos

    Check the selected color and size label against your order. Look at seams, zippers or outsoles. If a measurement matters, request a photo with a ruler while the seller’s return window is still open.

  4. Estimate the delivered cost

    Add the item price, domestic delivery, agent or payment fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes. Check whether your shipping line charges by actual or volumetric weight.

Frequently asked questions

What is an OopBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping reference that groups products by category so you can compare listings and decide which seller links to check with your agent. The product examples on this page come from HipobuyFind.

Where do the two shopping buttons lead?

Both buttons open the HipobuyFind homepage. Each catalog card opens its own HipobuyFind listing.

Does the displayed price include international shipping?

The displayed amount is an approximate USD item price from the catalog. Get the current item price and a separate shipping estimate before paying.

What if my agent cannot open a product link?

Open the HipobuyFind listing first and look for the original seller URL. Check whether your agent supports that marketplace. If the source listing is missing or inactive, check another product in the same category.
